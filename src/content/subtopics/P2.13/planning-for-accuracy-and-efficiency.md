<section class="note-panel"><h2>Design for Manufacture and Project Management</h2><div class="note-intro"><p>Manufacturing plans specify <strong>materials, dimensions, tolerances, processes, sequence of work, deadlines and quality checks</strong>. Good planning reduces delays and repeated work.</p></div><div class="study-card"><h3>Critical Path Analysis</h3><p><strong>Critical path analysis (CPA)</strong> maps tasks, their durations and their dependencies. Independent tasks can happen at the same time. The <strong>critical path</strong> is the sequence that determines the shortest possible completion time. Delaying a task on that path delays the project unless time is recovered elsewhere.</p></div><div class="study-card"><h3>Scrum</h3><p><strong>Scrum</strong> organises collaborative work into short periods called <strong>sprints</strong>. The team agrees on work to complete, reviews progress frequently and uses feedback to adjust the next sprint. This supports rapid responses to problems or changing requirements.</p></div></section>
