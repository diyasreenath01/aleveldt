<div class="note-grid"><div class="study-card"><h3>Simulation</h3>
<p>A CAD/CAM simulation is a virtual run-through of a manufacturing process that allows designers to evaluate feasibility before production.</p>
<p><strong>Advantages</strong></p>
<p>Identifies collisions, tool clashes, and axis limit errors</p>
<p>Predicts machining time and production efficiency</p>
<p>Enables nesting to reduce material waste</p>
<p>Assesses 3D print quality and support material requirements</p></div>
<div class="study-card"><h3>Virtual modelling</h3>
<p>Virtual modelling uses 3D CAD and simulation software to test product performance before manufacture.</p>
<p>Checks fit, clearance, and movement of components</p>
<p>Simulates CAM processes to prevent machining errors</p>
<p>Allows production system planning involving FMS cells, conveyors, robots, and AGVs</p></div>
<div class="study-card"><h3>Finite element analysis (FEA)</h3>
<p>FEA divides a CAD model into a <strong>mesh of small elements</strong> and uses calculations to predict how it responds to forces.</p>
<p>It can analyse <strong>stress, strain and deformation</strong>, identify likely failure points and help designers optimise a product before physical testing.</p>
<p>Examples include crash simulation and structural testing. Results depend on the accuracy of the digital model and require specialist knowledge to interpret.</p></div>
<div class="study-card"><h3>Computational fluid dynamics (CFD)</h3>
<p>CFD simulates the movement of <strong>air, liquids and heat</strong> around or through a CAD model.</p>
<p>It can be used for vehicle aerodynamics, airflow and thermal analysis, allowing designs to be improved before manufacture and reducing reliance on physical tests such as wind tunnels.</p></div>
<div class="study-card"><h3>Virtual reality (VR)</h3>
<p>VR can link to a 3D CAD model so designers or users can experience a digital prototype at close to real scale.</p>
<p>It is useful for <strong>ergonomic testing, spatial evaluation and user interaction</strong>, helping identify design problems before a physical prototype is made.</p>
<p><strong>Limitations:</strong> specialist hardware can be expensive and the virtual model must accurately represent the real product.</p></div>
<div class="study-card"><h3>Why advanced simulation matters</h3>
<p>FEA, CFD and VR allow more design decisions to be tested digitally before manufacture. This can reduce development time and the number of physical prototypes, while helping designers identify problems earlier.</p></div></div></section>
