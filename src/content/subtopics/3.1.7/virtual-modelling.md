<div class="note-grid"><div class="study-card"><h3>Simulation</h3>
<p>A CAD/CAM simulation is a virtual run-through of a manufacturing process that allows designers to evaluate feasibility before production.</p>
<p><strong>Advantages</strong></p>
<p>Identifies collisions, tool clashes, and axis limit errors</p>
<p>Predicts machining time and production efficiency</p>
<p>Enables nesting to reduce material waste</p>
<p>Assesses 3D print quality and support material requirements</p></div>
<div class="study-card"><h3>Virtual modelling (A-level only)</h3>
<p>Virtual modelling uses 3D CAD and simulation software to test product performance before manufacture.</p>
<p>Checks fit, clearance, and movement of components</p>
<p>Simulates CAM processes to prevent machining errors</p>
<p>Allows production system planning involving FMS cells, conveyors, robots, and AGVs</p></div>
<div class="study-card"><h3>Computational fluid dynamics (CFD)</h3>
<p>CFD is a 3D simulation tool used to analyse fluid and gas flow around products.</p>
<p>Used to assess aerodynamic performance (e.g. vehicle drag)</p>
<p>Results inform design improvements</p>
<p>Reduces reliance on physical testing such as wind tunnels</p></div>
<div class="study-card"><h3>Finite element analysis (FEA)</h3>
<p>FEA analyses stress and strain within CAD models.</p>
<p>Simulates forces, vibration, and shock loading</p>
<p>Highlights weak points using colour-coded stress maps</p>
<p>Enables optimisation through repeated testing</p>
<p>Used for both static and dynamic testing of products</p></div></div></section>
