<div class="study-card"><h4>Benefits</h4></div>
<div class="study-card"><h4>Fast and automated production</h4></div>
<div class="study-card"><h4>Reduces cost compared with traditional modelling</h4>

<p><strong>Quick prototype</strong> = manual materials (foam, card)</p></div>
<div class="study-card"><h3>Fused deposition modelling (FDM) / 3D printing</h3>
<p>CAD models are sliced into layers and built layer by layer</p>
<p>Uses thermoplastic filament and removable support material</p>
<p>Suitable for bespoke and one-off production</p>
<p>Metal 3D printing produces complex parts with no weak joints</p>
<p>Common materials include ABS, titanium, stainless steel, aluminium</p></div></div></section>
