<div class="note-grid"><div class="note-group-title"><h3>Rapid prototyping</h3></div>
<div class="study-card"><h4>Benefits</h4>
<p>Fast and automated production</p>
<p>Reduces cost compared with many traditional modelling methods</p>
<p>Can produce functional models, tooling and moulds</p></div>
<div class="study-card"><h3>STL and slicing</h3>
<p>An <strong>STL file</strong> converts the surface of a 3D CAD model into a mesh of triangular facets. Smaller triangles give a more detailed representation but create a larger file.</p>
<p><strong>Slicing software</strong> divides the model into layers and converts the digital geometry into machine instructions for layer-by-layer manufacture.</p></div>
<div class="study-card"><h3>Fused deposition modelling (FDM) / 3D printing</h3>
<p>CAD models are sliced into layers and built layer by layer</p>
<p>Uses thermoplastic filament and removable support material</p>
<p>Suitable for bespoke, one-off and prototype production</p>
<p>Rapid prototyping allows complex geometry to be produced directly from digital data without conventional moulds or tooling</p></div></div></section>
