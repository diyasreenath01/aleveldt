## Polymer-based sheet and film

Polymer sheet and film are supplied in thin, flat stock forms. Their suitability depends on properties such as **flexibility, toughness, transparency, chemical resistance, barrier properties, printability and ability to be formed or sealed**.

### Sheet

Polymer sheet is thicker and more rigid than film. It can be cut, line bent, thermoformed or vacuum formed into products such as guards, displays, trays and housings.

Examples include **acrylic (PMMA)** where transparency and surface appearance are important, and **HIPS** where economical thermoforming is required.

### Film

Polymer film is thin and flexible. It is widely used for bags, wraps, labels and flexible packaging. Materials such as **LDPE, PP and PET** can be selected according to the required flexibility, clarity, strength and barrier performance.

### Packaging considerations

Polymer film can protect products from moisture and contamination while adding very little mass. It can also be printed and heat sealed. However, multi-layer films and mixed materials can be difficult to separate and recycle.

### Useful comparison

**Rigid sheet** is chosen when the product needs shape and structural support. **Film** is chosen when flexibility, low mass and wrapping or sealing are more important.

In an exam, link the specific polymer and stock form to the product rather than simply saying 'plastic'.
