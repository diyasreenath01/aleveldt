<div class="note-grid"><div class="study-card"><h3>Sub-assemblies</h3>
<p>Pre-manufactured units incorporated into final products.</p>
<p>Enable concurrent manufacture, speeding up production.</p>
<p>Reduce final assembly complexity.</p>
<p><strong>Example:</strong> Typical sub-assemblies include bicycle braking systems, gear mechanisms, and drawer runners in furniture, which can be manufactured separately and fitted during final assembly.</p></div></div></section>
