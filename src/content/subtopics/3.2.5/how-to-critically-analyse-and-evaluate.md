<section class="note-panel"><h2>Critical Analysis and Evaluation</h2><div class="note-intro"><p><strong>Critical analysis</strong> is an objective study of a design’s strengths and weaknesses. It considers its purpose, users, materials, manufacture, usability and safety.</p><p><strong>Evaluation</strong> judges how well a design meets its specification. It should use evidence from testing. For example, if users struggle to open a box, the designer should identify why and test a different handle or opening mechanism.</p></div></section>
