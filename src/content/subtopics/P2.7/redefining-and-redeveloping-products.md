<section class="note-panel"><h2>Redefining and Redeveloping Products</h2><div class="study-card"><h3>Extension Strategies</h3><p>An <strong>extension strategy</strong> attempts to delay decline by improving performance, changing the appearance, adding features or releasing an updated version.</p></div><div class="study-card"><h3>Demand Pull and Technology Push</h3><p><strong>Demand pull</strong> occurs when customer needs encourage a new or improved product. <strong>Technology push</strong> occurs when a new technology enables a product that a company then introduces to the market. Technology push can fail if customers do not find the product useful or consider it too expensive.</p></div><div class="study-card"><h3>Planned Obsolescence</h3><p><strong>Planned obsolescence</strong> is the deliberate design of a product so that it needs replacing sooner than necessary. Unavailable spare parts or loss of software compatibility can contribute to this. It can increase sales but also creates waste and costs consumers money.</p></div></section>
