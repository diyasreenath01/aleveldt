<section class="note-panel"><h2>Product Directives and Environmental Requirements</h2><div class="note-intro"><p>Designers must identify the legal and environmental requirements that apply to their specific product and market.</p></div><div class="study-card"><h3>RoHS</h3><p><strong>RoHS</strong> restricts specified hazardous substances in electrical and electronic equipment.</p></div><div class="study-card"><h3>WEEE</h3><p><strong>WEEE</strong> concerns the separate collection and treatment of waste electrical and electronic equipment. Its <strong>crossed-out wheeled bin</strong> means the item should not go into ordinary household waste.</p></div><div class="study-card"><h3>Batteries, Packaging and Vehicles</h3><ul><li><strong>Battery requirements</strong> address hazardous substances, safe disposal and the collection and recycling of batteries.</li><li><strong>Packaging waste requirements</strong> encourage reduced packaging, recovery and recycling.</li><li><strong>End-of-life vehicle requirements</strong> encourage vehicle designs that allow parts and materials to be recovered when a vehicle is scrapped.</li></ul></div></section>
