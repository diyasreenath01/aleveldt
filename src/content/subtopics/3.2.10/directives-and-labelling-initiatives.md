<section class="note-panel"><h2>Product Directives and Environmental Requirements</h2><div class="note-intro"><p>Designers must identify the legal and environmental requirements that apply to their specific product and market. AQA expects knowledge of important directives, material identification systems and environmental labelling initiatives.</p></div><div class="study-card"><h3>RoHS</h3><p><strong>RoHS (Restriction of Hazardous Substances)</strong> restricts specified hazardous substances in electrical and electronic equipment. It influences material and component selection so that harmful substances are reduced in products placed on the market.</p></div><div class="study-card"><h3>WEEE</h3><p><strong>WEEE</strong> concerns the separate collection and treatment of waste electrical and electronic equipment. Its <strong>crossed-out wheeled bin</strong> means the item should not go into ordinary household waste and should enter an appropriate collection/recycling route.</p></div><div class="study-card"><h3>Batteries and packaging</h3><ul><li><strong>Battery requirements</strong> address hazardous substances, safe disposal and the collection and recycling of batteries.</li><li><strong>Packaging requirements</strong> encourage manufacturers to reduce unnecessary packaging and increase recovery and recycling.</li><li><strong>Polymer identification codes</strong> identify types of plastic to help sorting and recycling.</li></ul></div><div class="study-card"><h3>Energy ratings</h3><p>Energy ratings communicate the energy efficiency of products and help consumers compare products. This can influence designers to reduce energy consumption during the use stage of a product's life cycle.</p></div><div class="study-card"><h3>FSC — Forest Stewardship Council</h3><p>The <strong>FSC label</strong> is used on timber, paper and board products associated with responsibly managed forest sources and controlled supply chains. For designers, choosing FSC-certified material provides evidence that environmental and social considerations have been considered when sourcing forest-based materials.</p><p>In an exam, link FSC to <strong>sustainable sourcing</strong>, responsible forestry and helping consumers identify products made using responsibly sourced wood or paper.</p></div><div class="study-card"><h3>Other eco-labelling initiatives</h3><ul><li><strong>Mobius Loop:</strong> widely used recycling symbol; depending on its use it can indicate that a product or packaging is recyclable or contains recycled material.</li><li><strong>European Ecolabel:</strong> identifies products meeting environmental criteria across their life cycle.</li><li><strong>Energy labels:</strong> communicate energy efficiency to consumers.</li><li><strong>ENERGY STAR:</strong> identifies energy-efficient products that meet the programme's criteria.</li></ul></div></section>
