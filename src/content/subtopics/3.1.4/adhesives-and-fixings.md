<section class="note-panel"><h2>Adhesives and Fixings</h2><div class="note-intro"><p>Select an adhesive or fixing according to the <strong>materials, joint shape, required strength, environment, appearance and whether dismantling is needed</strong>.</p></div></section>
<section class="note-panel"><h2>Adhesives</h2><div class="note-grid"><div class="study-card"><h3>PVA</h3><p>A water-based adhesive used mainly with porous materials.</p><ul><li><strong>Best for:</strong> wood and manufactured boards.</li><li><strong>Advantages:</strong> easy to spread, dries clear and gives a strong timber bond when clamped.</li><li><strong>Limitation:</strong> unsuitable for most non-porous metals and plastics.</li></ul></div><div class="study-card"><h3>Epoxy resin</h3><p>A two-part resin and hardener adhesive.</p><ul><li><strong>Best for:</strong> strong bonding of different materials including metals, polymers and woods.</li><li><strong>Advantages:</strong> strong, waterproof and able to fill small gaps.</li><li><strong>Limitation:</strong> must be mixed correctly and allowed to cure.</li></ul></div><div class="study-card"><h3>Contact adhesive</h3><p>Applied to both surfaces and allowed to become tacky before they are pressed together.</p><ul><li><strong>Best for:</strong> large surface areas such as decorative laminates.</li><li><strong>Advantage:</strong> bonds quickly once the surfaces meet.</li><li><strong>Limitation:</strong> repositioning is difficult.</li></ul></div><div class="study-card"><h3>UV-hardening adhesive</h3><p>A clear adhesive that cures rapidly when exposed to ultraviolet light.</p><ul><li><strong>Best for:</strong> small accurate joints where a clear finish is useful.</li><li><strong>Advantage:</strong> parts can be positioned before rapid curing.</li></ul></div><div class="study-card"><h3>Solvent cement</h3><p>Solvent softens compatible thermoplastic surfaces so they fuse as the solvent evaporates.</p><ul><li><strong>Best for:</strong> close-fitting joints in compatible plastics such as acrylic, ABS and PVC.</li><li><strong>Advantage:</strong> can create neat permanent joints; low-viscosity cement can flow by capillary action.</li></ul></div></div></section>
<section class="note-panel"><h2>Choosing an adhesive</h2><div class="note-intro"><p>Do not choose from strength alone. Consider whether the surfaces are porous, the size of the bond area, exposure to moisture or heat, curing time, appearance and whether the materials are the same or different.</p></div></section>
