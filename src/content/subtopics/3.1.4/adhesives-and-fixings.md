<section class="note-panel"><h2>Adhesives and fixings</h2>
<div class="study-card"><h3>PVA</h3><ul><li>Polyvinyl acetate is water based and is suited to porous materials, particularly woods.</li><li>It is not suitable for non-porous plastics and metals.</li><li>It dries clear. Surfaces should be slightly rough and clean before application.</li><li>Excess glue should be wiped away because it can stain.</li><li>Pressure must be applied while it dries. The source gives a drying time of approximately 4–14 hours.</li></ul></div>
<div class="study-card"><h3>Epoxy resin</h3><ul><li>Can bond metals, plastics and woods and is waterproof.</li><li>Can bridge a small gap between materials and is suited to relatively small areas.</li><li>Equal amounts of resin and hardener are mixed and applied with a spreader.</li><li>The joint is held together while it dries. The source gives a drying time of approximately half an hour to six hours.</li></ul></div>
<div class="study-card"><h3>Contact adhesive</h3><ul><li>Can be used with metals, plastics and woods and is waterproof.</li><li>Useful for large flat sheets such as laminate flooring.</li><li>Apply it to both surfaces and allow it to become tacky before pressing the surfaces firmly together.</li><li>It bonds immediately once the surfaces are pressed together, so repositioning is difficult.</li></ul></div>
<div class="study-card"><h3>UV hardening adhesive</h3><ul><li>A clear, low-viscosity liquid that cures when exposed to UV light.</li><li>Contains a photoinitiator that causes it to harden when it absorbs UV radiation.</li><li>The pieces are positioned together, adhesive is placed in the joint and UV light is applied.</li><li>Can join woods, metals and plastics and is suited to relatively small areas.</li></ul></div>
<div class="study-card"><h3>Solvent cement</h3><ul><li>A clear solvent adhesive used for plastics such as acrylic, ABS and PVC.</li><li>The surfaces are placed together without gaps.</li><li>A syringe or small brush can introduce it into the joint by capillary action.</li><li>The solvent softens the surfaces so they join together.</li></ul></div>
<div class="study-card"><h3>Jigs and fixtures</h3><p>A <strong>jig</strong> helps parts or components to be produced repeatedly, quickly and accurately. It holds work at the correct angle or position and can guide a tool, reducing repeated marking out.</p><p>A <strong>fixture</strong> holds work in a specific position while a process is carried out, but does not guide the tool.</p></div></section>
