<section class="note-panel"><h2>Third-Party Feedback</h2><div class="note-intro"><p><strong>Third-party feedback</strong> comes from people independent of the design team and can reduce bias.</p></div><div class="study-card"><h3>Focus Groups</h3><p>A <strong>focus group</strong> brings together people from the target market to discuss or use a product. It may reveal problems with comfort, controls, appearance or price.</p><p>The participants should represent the intended users. Feedback is most valuable when it leads to a specific change that can be tested again.</p></div></section>
