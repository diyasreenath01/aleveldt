<section class="note-panel"><h2>Wood enhancement</h2>
<div class="note-intro"><p>Wood is enhanced to improve durability, stability, strength, appearance, and resistance to environmental damage, particularly for outdoor or structural use.</p></div>
<div class="note-grid"><div class="note-group-title"><h3>Wood enhancement methods</h3></div>
<div class="study-card"><h4>Resins and laminations</h4>
<p>Used in engineered woods to enhance the usable parts of trees such as chips, fibres, and sawdust.</p>
<p>Chipboard is made by compressing wood chips with resin (e.g. urea formaldehyde).</p>
<p>Produces boards that are stable, uniform in strength, and less affected by humidity and temperature.</p></div>
<div class="study-card"><h4>Resins with fire retardants</h4>
<p>Resin is impregnated with fire-retardant chemicals to reduce flammability.</p>
<p>Used in applications such as indoor flooring and cladding.</p></div>
<div class="study-card"><h4>Laminations</h4>
<p>Thin veneers bonded to the surface of boards to improve appearance.</p>
<p>Allow lower-cost materials to resemble solid hardwood.</p></div>
<div class="study-card"><h4>Preservatives</h4>
<p>Protect wood, especially outdoors, from fungal and insect attack.</p>
<p>Copper-based preservatives penetrate deeply and protect the entire plank.</p></div>
<div class="study-card"><h4>Pigments</h4>
<p>Added to preservatives to change colour and improve appearance.</p>
<p>Can be used to make softwoods resemble hardwoods.</p></div>
<div class="study-card"><h4>Fire-retardant preservatives</h4>
<p>Used to pressure-treat wood for joists, cladding, and exhibition structures.</p>
<p>Reduce flammability and can increase surface hardness for flooring.</p></div>
<div class="study-card"><h4>Modified natural polysaccharides</h4>
<p>Chemicals impregnated into wood that cure within the cell structure.</p>
<p>Increase hardness, toughness, and dimensional stability.</p>
<p><strong>Structural composite lumber (SCL) and laminated veneer lumber (LVL)</strong></p>
<p>Manufactured by layering wood strands or veneers with resin and curing under heat and pressure.</p>
<p>Produce stable sections with fewer defects such as warping or splitting.</p>
<p>Used in beams, joists, and rafters due to high load-bearing capacity.</p></div></div></section>
