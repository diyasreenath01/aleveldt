## Registered designs

**Registered designs** provide legal protection for the appearance and decoration of a product rather than its working parts.

- A registered design can protect features of a product's appearance, helping prevent unauthorised copying of its aesthetic design.
- Registration is made through the **Intellectual Property Office (IPO)** by submitting the required representations of the design and paying a fee.
- Protection can last for up to **25 years**, subject to renewal requirements.
- Registered design protection is particularly important where a product's distinctive appearance contributes to its commercial value and brand identity.
- This differs from a **patent**, which protects a new invention or technical feature rather than simply the product's appearance.

The notes use counterfeit designer products as an example of why legal protection of appearance can be commercially important.
