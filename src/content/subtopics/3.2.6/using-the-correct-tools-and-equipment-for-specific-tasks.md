<section class="note-panel"><h2>Tools, Prototypes and Production Methods</h2><div class="note-intro"><p>The appropriate tools and processes depend on the <strong>material, shape, accuracy, quantity, available equipment and cost</strong>.</p></div><div class="study-card"><h3>Visual Prototype</h3><p>Shows the shape, size and appearance; it may not work.</p></div><div class="study-card"><h3>Proof of Concept Prototype</h3><p>Tests whether the main mechanism or technical idea works; it may look unlike the final product.</p></div><div class="study-card"><h3>Production Prototype</h3><p>Closely represents how the manufactured product will look and function; it supports final testing and user trials.</p></div></section>
