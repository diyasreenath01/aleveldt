<section class="note-panel"><h2>Ensuring your own safety and that of others</h2>
<div class="note-intro"><p>Health and safety is everyone’s responsibility and employers have a duty of care to ensure that procedures are implemented to keep employees safe, and to prevent them from being harmed or becoming ill through work.</p></div>
<div class="study-card"><h3>Employers should ensure</h3><ul>
<li>The workplace is safe and free from risks to health, such as use of extraction systems to remove harmful dust or fumes.</li>
<li>Welfare facilities such as first aid are provided.</li>
<li>Dangerous items and substances are used and stored safely, and removed from use if necessary.</li>
<li>Training, information and adequate supervision are provided as necessary.</li>
<li>Personal protective equipment (PPE) is provided.</li>
<li>Machinery has guards in place, is safe to use, maintained and in good working order.</li>
<li>Appropriate safety signage is provided.</li>
<li>Accident reporting systems are in place, RIDDOR (Reporting of Injuries, Diseases and Dangerous Occurrences Regulations, 2013).</li>
</ul></div>
<div class="study-card"><h3>Employees should ensure</h3><ul>
<li>Take reasonable care for their own health and safety, as well as that of those in the vicinity.</li>
<li>Use the personal protective equipment (PPE) provided.</li>
<li>Use machinery in accordance with the given training instructions.</li>
<li>Report any accidents, near miss or dangerous event.</li>
</ul></div>
<div class="study-card"><h3>General safe working practices</h3><ul>
<li>Do not operate any machinery or equipment unless you have been trained in its use.</li>
<li>PPE must be worn when using machinery.</li>
<li>All guards and safety measures should be utilised.</li>
<li>Work areas should be kept neat, clean and free of hazards. Spillages should be reported.</li>
<li>All persons must be aware of emergency procedures, such as fire evacuation routes, locations of fire extinguishers and emergency stops.</li>
<li>Walkways should be kept clear and free from trip hazards.</li>
<li>If a person becomes ill or injured or in the event of an accident, this should be reported to a supervisor or teacher.</li>
</ul></div></section>
