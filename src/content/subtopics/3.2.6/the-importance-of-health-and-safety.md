<section class="note-panel"><h2>The importance of health and safety</h2>
<div class="study-card"><h3>Health and Safety Executive (HSE)</h3><p>The Health and Safety Executive (HSE) is a national independent watchdog for work-related health, safety and illness.</p><ul><li>The HSE helps companies and employers to meet health and safety obligations.</li><li>They produce guidance documents and investigate accidents in the workplace.</li></ul></div>
<div class="study-card"><h3>Health and Safety at Work Act (1974)</h3><ul>
<li>The Health and Safety at Work Act (HSWA) is the main piece of legislation for British health and safety law.</li>
<li>To comply, all employers must ensure, so far as is reasonably practicable, that all employees and visitors to the workplace are protected in terms of health, safety and welfare.</li>
<li>Compliance includes assessing risks and adopting appropriate safety measures to provide a safe environment for product manufacture.</li>
</ul></div>
<div class="study-card"><h3>Control of Substances Hazardous to Health regulations (2002)</h3><ul>
<li>To comply with COSHH employers need to prevent, reduce or control their workers’ exposure, as well as any workers in the vicinity, to substances that may be hazardous or cause ill health.</li>
<li>Hazardous substances include paints, varnishes, cleaning agents and adhesives that may be used during work.</li>
<li>They also include fumes created by processes such as soldering or brazing, and airborne particles such as dust created by cutting or sanding timber.</li>
<li>Hazardous substances will usually show a symbol that meets the classification labelling and packaging (CLP) regulations.</li>
<li>CLP hazard pictograms allow easy identification of a hazard as corrosive, flammable, toxic, etc.</li>
</ul></div>
<div class="study-card"><h3>Additional industrial measures</h3><ul>
<li>Where there is movement of goods around a factory floor, there may be designated safe zones or walkways that must be kept free from machinery.</li>
<li>Forklift trucks or vehicles will usually be fitted with a flashing light and/or reverse signal alarm to alert those near the vehicle movements.</li>
</ul></div></section>
