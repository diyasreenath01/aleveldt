## Development of designs

A design intended for manufacture normally develops from an initial proposal through modelling and prototyping before it becomes a production product.

### From prototype to production

A **visual prototype** communicates overall form, size and appearance and may contain no working parts.

A **proof-of-concept prototype** tests whether the main function or technical principle works.

A **production prototype** closely represents the intended manufactured product and can be used for final testing and user trials.

When moving from a prototype to batch or mass production, designers may need to alter materials, dimensions, tolerances, joints and manufacturing methods so the product can be produced repeatedly, accurately and economically.

The final manufacturing process must suit the **material, shape, required accuracy, production quantity and cost**.
