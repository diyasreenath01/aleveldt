<section class="note-panel"><h2>Quality Assurance</h2><div class="note-intro"><p><strong>Quality assurance (QA)</strong> consists of planned procedures that <strong>prevent defects</strong>. Examples include checking suppliers, reviewing drawings, maintaining machines and training workers.</p><p><strong>QA plans for quality; QC checks whether the required quality has been achieved.</strong></p></div><div class="study-card"><h3>Total Quality Management</h3><p><strong>Total quality management (TQM)</strong> involves the whole workforce in continuous improvement. Workers report problems and help improve processes so products are made correctly the first time.</p></div><div class="study-card"><h3>Six Sigma</h3><p><strong>Six Sigma</strong> reduces defects and variation using <strong>DMAIC</strong>: <strong>Define</strong> the problem, <strong>Measure</strong> it, <strong>Analyse</strong> its causes, <strong>Improve</strong> the process and <strong>Control</strong> the improvement.</p></div></section>
