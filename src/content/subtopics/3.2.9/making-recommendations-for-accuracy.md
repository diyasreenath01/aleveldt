## Making recommendations for accuracy

Recommendations for accuracy should suit the **scale of production**, required tolerance, product risk and manufacturing process.

### One-off and prototype manufacture

For one-off products and prototypes, accuracy can be improved by:
- working from clear drawings and dimensions
- using a common datum
- accurate marking out
- using templates where shapes repeat
- checking dimensions with appropriate measuring equipment during manufacture.

### Batch production

For repeated batches, designers can recommend:
- jigs and fixtures to locate components consistently
- templates to remove repeated marking-out errors
- standardised components
- planned quality-control checks
- vernier calipers, micrometers or go/no-go gauges where appropriate.

### Large-scale and mass production

For high-volume manufacture, recommendations may include:
- CNC or automated equipment for repeatability
- dedicated tooling and fixtures
- automated or rapid inspection systems
- statistical sampling where appropriate
- clearly specified tolerances and quality-control points.

The most accurate method is not automatically the best choice. The required accuracy must be balanced against production speed, cost and the consequences of a component being outside tolerance.
