## Sub-assemblies

A **sub-assembly** is a pre-manufactured unit that is incorporated into a final product. Examples on a bicycle include a gear cassette, derailleur mechanism and braking system.

Manufacturers may make the main product themselves while buying specialist sub-assemblies from external suppliers. This can give access to specialist expertise and consistent components without manufacturing every part in-house.

Sub-assemblies can also be produced **concurrently** with the main product. For example, drawers can be manufactured while a cabinet is being made and fitted during final assembly. This reduces final assembly work and can shorten production time.

The benefit depends on reliable suppliers, compatible dimensions and consistent quality; a poor or delayed sub-assembly can disrupt the whole product.
