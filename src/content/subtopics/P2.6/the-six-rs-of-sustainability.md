<section class="note-panel"><h2>The Six Rs of Sustainability</h2><div class="study-card"><h3>The Six Rs</h3><ul><li><strong>Rethink:</strong> Consider whether the product or a different solution is needed.</li><li><strong>Refuse:</strong> Avoid unnecessary products, materials or packaging.</li><li><strong>Reduce:</strong> Use less material and energy.</li><li><strong>Reuse:</strong> Use a product or component again.</li><li><strong>Repair:</strong> Fix or replace parts to extend a product’s life.</li><li><strong>Recycle:</strong> Recover materials when the product can no longer be used.</li></ul><p>Designing out unnecessary material and extending a product’s life can reduce waste before recycling becomes necessary.</p></div></section>
