<section class="note-panel"><h2>Cultural Acceptability</h2><div class="study-card"><h3>Cultural Acceptability</h3><p>A product’s <strong>colour, imagery, wording, materials or function</strong> may be interpreted differently by different users. Religious beliefs and local customs can also affect whether a product is suitable.</p><p>Designers should research the intended market and test ideas with users rather than assume that one design will be accepted everywhere.</p></div></section>
