<h2>3. Disassembly</h2>
<div class="study-card"><h3>Disassembly (A-level only)</h3>
<p>Disassembly is the <strong>systematic separation of components</strong> for repair, reuse, recycling, or disposal at end of life.</p></div>
<div class="study-card"><h3>Design strategies that support disassembly</h3>
<p>Products can be designed for easier disassembly by:</p>
<p>Applying a <strong>‘cradle to cradle’</strong> approach, keeping material types separate<br> </p>
<p>Using <strong>easily released polymer snap fittings<br> </strong></p>
<p>Using <strong>standard fasteners</strong> (e.g. Pozidriv screws) that require common tools<br> </p>
<p>Designing in line with <strong>End of Life Vehicles (ELV)</strong> legislation<br> </p>
<p>Using <strong>smart materials</strong> (e.g. SMA, SMP) to enable automatic or ‘active’ disassembly</p></div>
