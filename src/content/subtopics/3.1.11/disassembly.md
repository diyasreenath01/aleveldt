<section class="note-panel"><h2>3. Disassembly</h2>
<div class="note-grid"><div class="study-card"><h3>Disassembly (A-level only)</h3>
<p>Disassembly is the <strong>systematic separation of components</strong> for repair, reuse, recycling, or disposal at end of life.</p></div>
<div class="note-group-title"><h3>Design strategies that support disassembly</h3></div>
<div class="study-card"><h4>Products can be designed for easier disassembly by:</h4>
<p>Applying a <strong>‘cradle to cradle’</strong> approach, keeping material types separate<br> </p></div>
<div class="study-card"><h4>Using easily released polymer snap fittings</h4>
<p>Using <strong>standard fasteners</strong> (e.g. Pozidriv screws) that require common tools<br> </p></div>
<div class="study-card"><h4>Designing in line with End of Life Vehicles (ELV) legislation</h4>
<p>Using <strong>smart materials</strong> (e.g. SMA, SMP) to enable automatic or ‘active’ disassembly</p></div></div></section>
