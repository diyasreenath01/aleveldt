<section class="note-panel"><h2>Responsible Design</h2><div class="note-intro"><p><strong>Responsible design</strong> considers environmental, social and economic effects from raw material extraction through manufacture, use and disposal.</p></div><div class="study-card"><h3>Materials and Packaging</h3><p>Material selection should consider whether a resource is finite, how much energy processing requires, transport distances, product lifespan and whether the material can be reused or recycled.</p><p>Packaging must protect and preserve a product without using unnecessary material. Designers can reduce its weight and volume, use recycled content, and make it reusable or recyclable.</p></div><div class="study-card"><h3>Carbon Footprint</h3><p>A product's <strong>carbon footprint</strong> includes greenhouse gas emissions associated with its materials, manufacture, transport, use and disposal. Looking only at factory emissions gives an incomplete picture.</p></div><div class="study-card"><h3>Whole-life evaluation</h3><p>A product that is lighter or uses advanced technology is not automatically more sustainable. A lightweight composite vehicle component may reduce fuel or energy use during its working life, but the composite may require energy-intensive manufacture and be difficult to separate and recycle. A rechargeable electronic product may avoid repeated disposable batteries, but a sealed battery can shorten product life if it cannot be economically replaced.</p><p>The better environmental choice therefore depends on the <strong>whole lifecycle</strong>: extraction, manufacture, transport, energy used in service, durability, repair, reuse and end-of-life recovery.</p></div><div class="study-card"><h3>Circular Economy</h3><p>A <strong>circular economy</strong> aims to keep products and materials in use for longer. Designers support this by making products <strong>durable, repairable, upgradeable and easy to disassemble</strong>. Parts can be reused and materials recovered when the product can no longer serve its original purpose.</p></div></section>
